# Code of Conduct

LabLLM uses the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume people are here to learn, build, and improve the project.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Giving clear, actionable feedback.
- Asking questions without dunking on beginners.
- Keeping criticism focused on code, design, docs, and behavior.
- Crediting people for their work.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, repeated disruption, or bad-faith arguing.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block people who violate this code of conduct.

Report problems by contacting the project owner directly on GitHub.
