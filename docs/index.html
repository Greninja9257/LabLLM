Tiny questions

Mostly practical answers.

Does it train real models?

Yes. It builds GPT-style decoder models with MLX and saves checkpoints you can load back into the app.

Is this for giant frontier models?

No. It is for learning, experiments, small runs, dataset exploration, and local tinkering.

What Mac do I need?

macOS 14 or newer on Apple Silicon. More memory gives you more room for bigger experiments.

Can I contribute?

Yes. Open an issue, propose a small focused PR, or add yourself through the All Contributors flow.
